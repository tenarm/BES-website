# Video Asset: Order-to-Cash — End-to-End Flow

**Section:** Can be used as a supplementary hero video or "How It Works" enhancement
**Replaces:** The 3-step "How It Works" section could embed this
**Duration:** 30–40 seconds
**Dimensions:** 1280×720 (16:9)
**Type:** Screen recording with motion overlay annotations

## What to Show

A complete order-to-cash cycle in the actual TenArm BES interface, showing how one transaction flows seamlessly through every department:

### Scene 1: CRM — Lead Captured (0s–5s)
- CRM Pipeline view
- A deal card "Anand Steels · ₹28L" moves from "Lead" to "Qualified"
- Brief flash of the deal detail form

### Scene 2: Sales — Quote Created (5s–10s)
- Transition to Sales module
- Create Quotation: customer, line items (TMT bar 12mm · 200 MT · ₹14,000/MT)
- Click "Send to Customer"
- Status: Draft → Sent

### Scene 3: Sales — Order Confirmed (10s–15s)
- Customer accepts quote
- Quotation converts to Sales Order with one click
- Order number generated: SO-2026-0421
- **Annotation overlay:** "Inventory auto-reserved. Finance notified."

### Scene 4: Inventory — Stock Reserved (15s–20s)
- Automatic switch to Inventory view
- Stock bar for TMT 12mm drops from 1,240 MT to 1,040 MT
- "Reserved for SO-0421" tag appears
- **Annotation overlay:** "No manual data entry. Zero re-keying."

### Scene 5: Logistics — Dispatch (20s–25s)
- Dispatch screen shows the shipment
- Truck assignment, delivery challan generated
- Status: "Dispatched"

### Scene 6: Finance — Invoice & Payment (25s–35s)
- Invoice auto-generated from sales order
- GST calculated automatically
- Payment received → AR entry cleared
- P&L updates in real-time
- **Final annotation:** "One transaction. Five departments. Zero dropped batons."

## Visual Direction

- **Clean annotations** — Minimal text overlays in Inter font, appearing at key moments
- **Smooth transitions** — Cross-fade between modules, not hard cuts
- **Highlight the automation** — Use a subtle gold glow on actions that happen automatically
- **Real product UI** — No wireframes or mockups
